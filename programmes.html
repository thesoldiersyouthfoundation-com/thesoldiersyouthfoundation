I

Defence recruitment preparation

For Army (Agniveer), Navy and Air Force aspirants.

Physical trainingDaily endurance, strength, agility, running and drill.
Written exam coachingMaths, reasoning, GK and English, with current affairs updates.
Weekly mock testsFull-length mocks with progress tracking and doubt clearing.
DocumentationGuidance on forms, certificates and the selection process.
II

Youth skill development

For young people preparing for civilian work.

Computer skillsComputer basics, documentation and Excel.
Job readinessCV writing, interview practice and workplace communication.
Placement supportIntroductions to employers and internship partners.
III

Mentorship

Part of every programme.

Routines and goalsDaily discipline, goal planning and weekly accountability.
LeadershipSessions on resilience and leadership from veterans.
Community serviceVolunteering and social activities alongside veterans.

Who can apply

Age and educationIn line with the current recruitment rules for your target service. We check eligibility during counselling.
PriorityYoung people from rural and low-income families.
