Partner with us

Fund training that leads to careers in service.

We work with companies, foundations and individuals to train young people for the Armed Forces and skilled work, and we report on every rupee.

Founder and Chairman

Lt Col (DR) S. Srinivas Rao

33 years in the Indian Army in military training, logistics, security and crisis operations. He founded the Foundation to help young people from modest backgrounds reach the Armed Forces.

Why partner with us

Veteran-led

Training is designed and delivered by retired Armed Forces personnel who know the selection process first-hand.

Measurable outcomes

We track attendance, fitness progress, mock scores, selections and job placements for every trainee.

Registered society

Registered under the Telangana Societies Registration Act, 2001 (Reg. No. 94 of 2021). Registration details

Ways to fund

Choose an option below or talk to us about a programme built around your CSR goals.

Sponsor one trainee

Full training, kit and test series for one young person.

Sponsor a batch

A complete training batch, named after your organisation.

Kits and test series

Training kits, study material and mock test series.

Skills lab

Computers and equipment for the job readiness programme.

What you receive

  • Utilisation certificate for each financial year
  • Quarterly progress report with trainee outcomes
  • Photos and stories for your CSR and annual reports
  • Site visits to training sessions and passing-out events

Donate as an individual

Any amount helps cover kits, travel and test fees for a trainee.
