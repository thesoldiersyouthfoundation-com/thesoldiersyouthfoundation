Why we exist

Many capable young people miss out on Defence careers and stable jobs because they cannot afford coaching or have no one to guide them.

We close that gap with structured training, mentoring from veterans and practical skills, so every candidate arrives at selection fit, prepared and confident.

What we do

01

Defence recruitment preparation

Daily physical training, written exam coaching and weekly mock tests for Army (Agniveer), Navy and Air Force selection.

02

Youth skill development

Computer basics, communication, CV writing and interview practice, with placement support for those choosing civilian careers.

03

Mentorship

One-to-one guidance from veterans on discipline, goal setting and resilience, alongside community volunteering.
