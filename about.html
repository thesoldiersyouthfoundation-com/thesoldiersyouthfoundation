About us

Our story

The Soldiers Youth Foundation was registered in 2021 under the Telangana Societies Registration Act. It is led by veterans of the Indian Army.

We offer coaching, physical training and mentoring for young people from underprivileged and rural backgrounds who want to join the Armed Forces or find stable work.

Mission

To give every motivated young person the training, discipline and guidance to earn a place in the Armed Forces or in skilled work.

Values

Discipline, integrity and service to the nation and community.

Leadership

Served 33 years in the Indian Army in security leadership, military training, logistics and crisis operations.

  • Experience in VIP and industrial security, risk management and civil-military coordination.
  • PhD in Sports Coaching and Nutrition.
  • Mentors Agniveer aspirants and supports underprivileged youth into the Armed Forces.
